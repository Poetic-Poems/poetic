A Poem With Everything

A Poem With Everything
A Poet
Wednesday, 23 September 2026

[Stanza]
Four lines are plenty for a verse,
though this one carries extra freight:
a song beneath it, and, in reverse,
a note and gloss to contemplate.
recording on Suno
