**Severity:** Low

`test/blogger-auth.test.js` imports/tests only 6 of the 10 functions
`blogger-auth.js` exports (`:591-602`): `waitForCode`, `generateState`,
`generatePkce`, `describeBlogAccess`, `saveFileMode0600`, `promptHidden`.
`parseArgs` (line 88) — a small pure function with no I/O — is exported but
untested despite costing nothing to add. `exchangeCodeForTokens`,
`lookupBlogId`, and `listMyBlogs` (the network-calling helpers) are also
exported but untested, along with all of `main()` (consent-URL construction
with PKCE/state/scopes, the credential-prompt/save flow). Fresh coverage:
49.83% lines, 57.14% funcs.

**Why it matters.** Every security-sensitive primitive (PKCE, CSRF state,
masked prompt, atomic 0600 write) remains directly tested — this is a
one-off interactive script invoked from no CI workflow, so the practical
risk is low. But a regression in URL construction (missing a scope, a
malformed PKCE parameter) would only surface by a human noticing the OAuth
flow behave oddly, since nothing else would catch it.

**Suggested fix:** Add a test asserting the OAuth consent URL's query
parameters (state, PKCE challenge/method, scopes) are constructed
correctly, without mocking `fetch` or requiring a live OAuth round-trip.
Optional given this script's low usage frequency; convenient to do
alongside `TD-PPpoet-26080802` (F-SEC-01) or `TD-PPpoet-26080812`
(F-OPS-02), which also touch this file.
